The Company

Akeo Consulting is a small Software Development company established in the North-West of Ireland, in the breathtaking wilderness of County Donegal.

Our expertise ranges from FLOSS development — Rufus, BIOS and UEFI including ARM64 platforms, file system drivers, libwdi / Zadig, libusb — to Embedded Systems, Security, and much more.

Open Source

Firm believers in Free Software, we apply our 30+ years of expertise to FLOSS projects like Rufus — a widely trusted Windows application with millions of downloads every month.

We have also developed innovative solutions for long-standing computing problems: ubrx, UEFI:NTFS, Mosby. For more, please see our full list of GitHub repositories.
